Unit Three

Describing locations and giving directions

Introduction

When we want to talk about movement, direction and location of something or someone, we use prepositions that show direction and location. In this unit, you will use the words ‘towards’, ‘between’, ‘beside’, ‘from’, ‘into’, ‘onto’, ‘through’, and ‘across’ to describe location and direction of different objects. The competences developed will enable you to tell where something or someone is located in different communicative contexts.
