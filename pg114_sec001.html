Reading practice

Read the following example of a journal written by a Standard Five pupil to record things that inspired him on a TV programme.

Then, answer the questions that follow.

Date: 8th March 2025

Title: A memorable news item on a TV programme

Source: TV news about One Copy, One Pupil

What inspired me: Today, I heard the news that the government of the United Republic of Tanzania, through the Ministry of Education Science and Technology and the Tanzania Institute of Education, plans to produce enough copies so that every pupil will have a personal copy for each subject.

This news greatly inspired me.

It means I will have access to those books at school and home.

This will improve my understanding of the subjects.

My thoughts and feelings: I was very happy to hear the news.

I immediately shared it with my parents, who were also very excited.

Reflection: The news reminded me of the impact of two or three pupils sharing one copy and the importance of having a copy for each pupil.

Questions

1. What are the key components of the journal?

2. Why did the news about every pupil receiving a personal copy for each subject inspire the writer?

3. How does the pupil think having his or her own copy of each book will change the way he or she studies at home and school?

4. What challenges did she or he face when sharing books with other pupils, and how does this news solve challenges?
