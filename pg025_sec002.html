At the market

Last Saturday, Razia and her brother Mohamed went to the market.
Razia bought more oranges than Mohamed.
Mohamed bought more bananas than Razia.
The oranges were sweeter than the bananas.
They both had fun and Razia said that the market was more exciting than the supermarket.

Questions

1.Who bought more oranges?
2.Which fruit was sweeter than the other?
3.Where did Razia and Mohamed go?
4.What did Razia say about the market?
