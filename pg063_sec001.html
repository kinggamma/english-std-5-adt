English Pupil’s Book Standard Five

(b)

Split into two sentences the following sentences joined using neither… nor conjunction.

Example: Neither John nor Peter is at school today.

John is not at school today.

Peter is not at school today either.

1. Neither the dog nor the cat wants to eat.

2. She likes neither rice nor beans.

3. We saw neither the sun nor the moon.

4. Neither my brother nor my sister can swim.
