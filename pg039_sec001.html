(b) Read the following dialogue between Rehema and David. Pay attention to the words in bold.
Rehema:Hi! We’ve not met before, have we?
David:No, we haven’t.
Rehema:I thought it’d be good to know each other.
David:
Sure! My name is David. I have a brother; his name is Adam.
Rehema:
My name is Rehema. I have a sister; her name is Nuru. Nice to meet you, David.
David:
Nice to meet you, Rehema. Where do you live?
Rehema:
I live with my family in Dodoma. My parents bought a plot of land there.
David:Oh! Your parents must be rich, buying a plot of land in Dodoma!
Rehema:
Not really. They used their long-term savings.
David:
That’s cool then! My parents are also saving up to buy a piece of land one day.
Rehema:
That’s awesome! I hope its location will be in Dodoma, as well.
David:Ha ha How I wish!
Questions
1. Who says "my parents"?
2. What does "my" show?
3. What other words in the dialogue show possession?
