Using active and passive voices
Introduction

In everyday communication, the active voice focuses on the doer of the action rather than the receiver.Conversely, the passive voice shifts the focus to the receiver of the action rather than the doer.In this unit, you will explore using active and passive voices in everyday communication.You will practise transforming sentences from active to passive and passive to active voices.You will learn why and when we use the passive voice in communication.The competences developed will enable you communicate effectively and with clarity and flexibility in English.

Think about the words and sentences you use when you focus on telling what is done but not who does it.
