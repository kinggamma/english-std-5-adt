Summarising issues in the story listened to/observed

Speaking and listening/signing and observing practices

Listen to/observe the story about the following picture and answer the questions that follow.

The Ghost in the Room

One quiet night in the village of Mwandiga, a boy named Juma was sleeping. The wind blew gently and moonlight shone through the window. Suddenly, Juma heard a strange sound and saw something white floating in the corner of his room. Frightened, he wondered whether it was a ghost. Then the “ghost” sneezed. Juma switched on his flashlight and laughed: it was his sister’s white dress hanging on a chair and moving because the window was open. The sneeze came from his mother sleeping next door. Juma closed the window and went back to sleep, warm and happy.

Questions

1.What is the story about?
2.Who are the main characters in the story?
3.Where and when does the story take place?
4.What happened at the beginning of the story?
5.What happened in the middle of the story?
6.What happened in the end?
7.What problem did the characters face, and how did they solve it?
8.What lesson did you learn from the story?
9.What would you say if you had to tell a friend this story in a sentence?
