Reading practice

Read and act out the following dialogue.

Police Officer:Mwaluko, what has happened here?
Mwaluko:
I have seen Mashaka running away from my house with a bag.I have chased him while shouting for help.I suspect him of stealing my missing clothes.
Police Officer:
(Turning to Mashaka) Mashaka, have you stolen the clothes?
Mashaka:
(Nervously) I… I … I… have just borrowed them.I did not steal anything.
Witness:We have seen Mashaka running with the bag while this man was shouting for help.
Mwaluko:Yes! I found my clothes in his bag. He hid them!
Police Officer:
Mashaka, you’ve been caught with the evidence, and Mwaluko saw you running.Other witnesses have also seen you.Do you still deny it?
Mashaka:(Looking down) I… I have made a mistake.
Police Officer:You’ve put yourself in trouble. We’ve brought you here to write a statement of offence.
Mwaluko:I worked hard to buy those clothes. Why has he stollen them?
Police Officer:
We understand the situation.Mashaka, do you have anything to say for yourself?
Mashaka:
(Sighing) I did not think about the consequences.I’m sorry.
Mwaluko:You should have asked if you needed help.
Police Officer:
We’ve heard enough.Now, let’s write the statement.
Mashaka’s Father:
Mashaka!I’ve just heard what you have done.I thought you’ve gone to school this morning, but you went to steal from Mwaluko’s house!I warned you several times, but you have never changed!
Mashaka:
(Looking down) Father, I have made a big mistake.I’m sorry!
Mashaka’s Mother:
Oh, Mashaka!We provided you with everything you needed.We fed you well, bought you school uniform, and sent you to school.Why have you done this?What else do you want, my son? (Crying.)
Mashaka:
(Crying) I never thought about the pain I have caused.Please forgive me!
Police Officer:
Now, Mashaka will have to take responsibility for his actions.He has been caught with the evidence, so the court of law will teach him his lesson.
Mashaka:
(Crying) Why did I do that?I didn't listen to my parents.Now, I'm in trouble.

Questions

1. What has happened at Mwaluko’s house?

2. Who has been caught with Mwaluko’s clothes?

3. Why has Mashaka’s father become upset?

4. What crime has Mashaka committed, according to the police officer?

5. How have Mashaka’s behaviour affected his parents?
