Reading practice

(a) Read aloud/sign the following arithmetic statements. Then, say/sign whether they are true or false.

1.

Mary bought three packs of pencils. Each pack had fifty-five pencils. She said she had fifty pencils in total.

2.

Ali had one hundred and fifty-five shillings. He spent fifty shillings at the shop. He says he has one hundred and five shillings left.

3.

A teacher has ninety sweets to give to fifteen pupils equally. She says each pupil will get six sweets.

4.

Two boxes, each has one thousand and ten marbles. Amani says the boxes have two thousand two hundred and twenty marbles together.

5.

Zawadi is packing gift bags. She puts thirteen sweets in each of her ten bags. She says she packed one hundred and thirty sweets in total.

6.

There were thirty birds in a tree. Twelve flew away. Musa says sixteen are left.

7.

An arithmetic teacher says eighty-eight divided by eleven is eight.

8. Maria added fifty and sixty and got one hundred and twenty.

9. Neema is learning multiplication. She says twelve times twelve is one hundred and forty-four.

10. James says that fifty-four divided by nine is seven.
