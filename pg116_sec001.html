Writing a poem from your journal reflections

(a) Follow the steps below to write a poem from your journal reflections.

Step 1: Read the journal titled, A memorable news item on a TV programme (Written by a Standard Five pupil)

Step 2: Think about the main idea Ask yourself

  • (i) What happened in the journal?
  • (ii) How did the writer feel?

Example: The news was about ensuring that every pupil receives one copy of the textbook for each subject. The writer felt happy because of the ensured easier access to knowledge.

Step 3: Write the main idea in one sentence. Example: The main idea is that more book copies will enhance learning.

Step 4: Choose the title for your poem Pick a short title that matches the main idea. Example titles: ‘The positive move’, ‘Exciting news’, ‘Helping hand’

Step 5: Write your poem (3–4 line stanza)

  • (i) Use the main idea and your feelings.
  • (ii) Try to make it rhyme, but it’s okay if it doesn’t.
  • (iii) Use simple, clear words.

Step 6: Edit your poem

  • (i) Read it aloud.
  • (ii) Fix any spelling or punctuation errors.

(b) Write a poem from your journal.

Step 1: Go back to the journal you wrote. Step 2: Read your journal again. Step 3: Follow the same steps

  • (i) Write the main idea.
  • (ii) Choose a poem title.
  • (iii) Write 3–4 line stanza poem.
  • (iv) Edit your poem carefully.

Note that you should use words that show how you feel. You can also draw a picture to accompany your poem to stimulate creativity and enhance visualisation.
