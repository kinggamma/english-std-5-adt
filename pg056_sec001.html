(b) Read and act out the following dialogue between two schoolmates, Sadick and Anna, who have met at the school compound.

Sadick: Hello, Anna.

Anna: Hello, Sadick. How’re you doing?

Sadick: I’m doing great, Anna. How about you?

Anna: I’m fine.

Sadick: Oh, excuse me, Anna. Look, I started reading this book last month, but I still haven’t finished it. I'm trying to make a sentence about this situation, but I'm finding it difficult.

Anna: Oh, do you mean making a sentence about something you started in the past but have not finished yet?

Sadick: Yes, Anna. That’s precisely what I mean.

Anna: That’s good. You can say, “I have been reading this book since last month.” This means you started reading the book last month and are still reading it.

Or, you can say, “I have been reading this book for a month.” This means you have been reading the book for a month and are still reading it.

Sadick: Aha, thank you. I can now make sentences to express actions I started in the past but I haven’t yet finished them.

Anna: Great. Bye!

Sadick: Bye!
