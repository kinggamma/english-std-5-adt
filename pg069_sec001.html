(b) Read the table below. The statements in Column A are in the active voice and the same statements in Column B are in the passive voice.

S/N Column A Column B
1. The chef cooks the food. The food is cooked by the chef.
2. The dog chased the cat. The cat was chased by the dog.
3. The pupils answered the questions. The questions were answered by the pupils.
4. She is writing a letter. A letter is being written by her.
5. They will clean the room. The room will be cleaned by them.
