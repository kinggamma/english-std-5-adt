My friend and I

(c) Read the following passage and answer the questions that follow.

My name is Aika, and I have a brother who loves playing football. His name is Maori. We have a fierce dog named Faster. It is playful and energetic, and its tail is light. I also have a good friend named Chausiku. She lives in Burundi. Chausiku loves her country very much, just like I love mine. Chausiku and her family have a very beautiful house. Here is a picture of their house in Burundi.

The colours of their house are shiny and beautiful. Its compound is attractive, with green, pink, yellow, and white flowers that mix beautifully. Our house is also lovely, and I like it very much. Chausiku and I have the same goal. Our goal is to work hard in studies. Each one of us wants to be a leader in her country in the future.

Questions

1. What are the names of three characters in the passage?

2. What does Chausiku love?

3. How would you describe the house in Burundi?

4. What is Aika and Chausiku’s goal?
