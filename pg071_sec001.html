Mr Fungo and the community

Mr Fungo is a well-known person in our community, and everyone seems to like him. He is always there to help, and offers advice to make things better for everyone. He works hard as a community lorry driver. Even though his salary is not high, he supports other people in whatever way possible.

Some people call him ‘a man of the people’ because he always puts others first. Other people call him ‘a generous man’ because he is always giving, whether it is time, help, or advice. A lot of people even say he has a golden heart, meaning that he is very kind.

One day, something unexpected happened. While Mr Fungo was reversing his lorry, he accidentally damaged the village’s water tap. The water supply was cut off for a few days. People were disappointed but took it as a minor accident. Therefore, they were not angry with him. Mr Fungo promised to fix the tap the following Monday, and everyone believed him.

Some people in the community did not want others to think Mr Fungo had done something wrong. Thus, when others asked about the water shortage, the answers were as follows.

"The tap was damaged."

"The tap was damaged when the car was reversed."

"A mistake was made."

"The tap will be fixed on Monday."

In the end, everything worked out. Mr Fungo fulfilled his promise. The water service was restored, and everyone appreciated how Mr Fungo always tried to help. It was just an accident, but it showed how much the community trusted and respected him.
