(b) From the dialogue, write sentences that show how to use similes, metaphors, and personifications.

Example

The sun smiled down on the village – personification

Reading practice

Read the following stanza aloud and answer questions that follow.

The sun wakes up and smiles so brightly,

Like a golden ball, it gives us light.

Hope is a seed that starts so small,

But grows to be the strongest of all.

Questions

1. Which line compares the sun to something else using the word “like”?

2. What line says that hope is something else? What is it called?

3. Which line makes the sun act like a person? What does the sun do?

4. Why do you think the poet uses these kinds of special words (like comparing and pretending things are people)?
