Unit Six
Using conjunctions
Introduction

In communication, we connect words and concepts to build complex sentences conveying the intended meaning. In this unit, you will practise using conjunctions in oral and written texts. You will use the conjunctions ‘either…or’, ‘neithernor’ and ‘too…to’ to join sentences that show choices and comparison. The competences developed will enable you to express clearly and precisely alternatives, negatives, and limitations in communication.

Think about the words you use when expressing choices and comparisons.
