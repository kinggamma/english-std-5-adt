Presenting simple speeches about self and family members on familiar topics

Self-introduction

Read the following self-introduction by Zawadi Msafiri and answer the questions that follow.

“My name is Zawadi Msafiri.

I was born in May 2014.

I come from Lindi and now I live in Sinza Madukani.

I study at Mugabe Primary School.

I’m in Standard Five.

I like my school.

Thank you for listening”

Questions

1. What is the speaker’s name?

2. Where does she come from?

3. Which school does she go to?

4. How would you write this self-introduction, if you were you making it?
