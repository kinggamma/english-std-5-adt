Unit Eleven

Writing clear and concise messages

Introduction

Writing concise messages helps us to share our feelings and talk to others. We can write thank-you notes to show gratitude and appreciation and apologise when we make mistakes. We need to use correct grammar and vocabulary to write these messages correctly. In this unit, you will learn to write thank-you and apology notes. You will practise writing notes to teachers, friends, and parents. The competences developed will enable you to talk to others and share your feelings.

Think about how your parents or friends feel when you write them a note saying ‘thank you’ or ‘sorry’.

(i) Appreciation messages
