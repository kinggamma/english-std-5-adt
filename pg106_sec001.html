(ii) Thank-you note

Study the following thank-you note and answer the questions that follow.

1. Start with a greeting
2. Share your gratitude with specific examples
3. Include any details you would like the recipient to know
4. Close with any additional thoughts or information
5. End with a polite closing
Dear Teacher,
Thank you for always helping me learn and grow.Your kindness and patience make learning fun and exciting.I appreciate how you instruct us so clearly and encourage me to do my best.
I am grateful for all the time and effort you spend teaching us.You make school a happy place, and I feel lucky to have you as my teacher.
Thank you for everything you do!
Sincerely,Jabir
