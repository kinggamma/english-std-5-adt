Did you know?

When we talk or write, we sometimes want to show who is doing the action. If we want to show who does something we use the active sentence. Example, "The boy kicked the ball". Other times, we want to show what is being done or who receives the action. Then, we use the passive sentence. Example, "The ball was kicked by the boy".

Reading and writing practice

(a) Read the following texts in bubbles about fixing a computer to get ideas about the active and passive voices.

Active voice
Mapesa is fixing a computer.
Madam Mapendo taught Mapesa to fix computers.
The government pays Madam Mapendo to teach.
Passive voice
Oh! A computer is being fixed by Mapesa.
It seems Mapesa is skilful.
I didn’t know that Mapesa was taught by Madam Mapendo.
Did Mapesa pay her?
