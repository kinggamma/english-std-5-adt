Reading practice

Read the following passage and answer the questions that follow.

A visit to the village

Last Sunday, Asha and her brother, Juma, visited their grandmother in the village. To reach the village, they walked across a small bridge over the river. On the other side of the river, they saw a cow resting under a big tree. When they arrived at their grandmother’s house, they found her sitting on a mat. There was a basket placed between her and the fire. Asha put her bag on the bench and went into the kitchen to help with the cooking. Later that day, they walked to the farm and picked some fresh maize. Then, they returned home happily from the farm.

Questions

1. Where did Asha and Juma visit last Sunday?

2. What did they cross to get to the other side of the river?

3. Where was the cow resting?

4. What was placed between the grandmother and the fire?

5. Where did Asha put her bag?

6. Where did Asha go to help with the cooking?

7. What did they pick from the farm?
