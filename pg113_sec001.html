Creating a journal for recording inspiring things (at home, at school, from TV and radio programmes, etc.)

Read the following story to learn about writing a journal of important things.

Best friend

One day, two friends were walking through a large semi-desert area. They were having fun, but then they disagreed. One of the friends became so angry that he slapped the other one. The friend who was slapped felt hurt, but instead of saying anything, he wrote in the sand, “Today, my best friend slapped me on the face.”

They continued walking until they came across a large, muddy pond. The friend who had been slapped accidentally slipped and got stuck in the mud. He was nearly drowning. The other friend quickly threw a rope and pulled him out. After being rescued, he wrote on a big stone, “Today, my best friend saved my life.”

The friend who had slapped him asked, “When I hurt you, you wrote in the sand, and when I helped you, you wrote on the stone, why?”

The friend smiled and said, “When someone hurts us, we should write it in the sand, where the wind can blow it away. But when someone does something kind for us, we should write it where the wind cannot blow it away because kindness should never be forgotten.”

Questions

1.Why do you think the friend chose to write in the sand?
2.Why do you think the friend chose to write on the stone?
3.What can we learn from the way the friend treated both kind and unkind actions?
4.What does the story teach us about how we should handle things in life?
5.
Do you think writing a journal can help us remember good moments and forget bad ones? Explain how.
6.
If you were in the story, would you write in the sand or on the stone? Why?
7.Can writing our thoughts in a journal help us?
