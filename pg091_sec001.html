Unit Nine
Speaking English clearly and naturally
Introduction

Spoken language has special features that help people speak smoothly and naturally. In this unit, you will learn to speak clearly and naturally in English. You will practise connected speech like saying ‘they’ll’ instead of ‘they will’ and ‘there’s’ instead of ‘there is.’ You will also learn how sounds in words change or disappear when we speak quickly, like saying ‘next door’ as ‘nexdoor’ or ‘used to’ as ‘useto.’ Finally, you will practise using tag questions, like ‘You like reading, don’t you?’ or ‘She’s your friend, isn’t she?’ The competences developed will help you understand and speak English more naturally.

Think about how the sounds of words can change speech when we pronounce them quickly.

Practising connecting words in speaking

(a) Say the following sentences in the way they are said/Sign the following sentences.

  • 1. I had an assignment.
  • 2. They will come tomorrow.
  • 3. But they will not stay long.
  • 4. Let us talk about it.
  • 5. That is the best option.
  • 6. She is a bad girl.

When we speak words often join together. This is called connected speech. Let see how the above sentences sound when we say them naturally.

  • 1. I had an assignment. → Sounds like “I hadanassignment”.
  • 2. They will come tomorrow. → Becomes “They’ll come tomorrow”.
  • 3. But they won’t stay long → Becomes “But they won’t stay long”.
  • 4. Let us talk about it. → Sounds like “Let’s takaboutit”.
  • 5. That is the best option. → Sounds like “That’s the bestoption”.
  • 6. She is a bad girl. → Sounds like “Sheez a bad girl”.
