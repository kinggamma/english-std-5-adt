Using ‘either … or’

Study the pictures, and read the questions and the statements. What can the boy choose to eat?

The boy can choose to eat either a banana or an apple.

What can the girl choose to wear?

The girl can choose to wear either a red or a blue blouse.

Did you know?

The conjunction "eitheror" suggests making one choice between two things, persons, or conditions. For example, "Either Baraka or Zawadi will clean the window" means one of these two will clean the window.
