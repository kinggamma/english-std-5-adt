Reading practice

Read the following sentences and describe the choices in each sentence.

Example: Janeth needs either a pen or a pencil to do her homework.

Description: The first choice is ‘a pen’ and the second choice is ‘a pencil.’

  1. 1. She can either go away or call the police, I don’t care.

  2. 2. It is either green or yellow. I don’t remember well.

  3. 3. You can stay with either me or Janet.

  4. 4. I go to either the library or the playground.

  5. 5. You can eat either rice or ugali.

  6. 6. I’ve taken some money to buy either an orange or a mango.

  7. 7. The keys are either on the hook or in the drawer.
