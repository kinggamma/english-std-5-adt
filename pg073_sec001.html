Unit Eight

Listening/observing for comprehension

Introduction

Listening/observing for comprehension is essential when learning English. It means interpreting, understanding, and responding to what you hear/observe. In this unit, you will respond to oral/signed instructions by performing different tasks. You will also listen to/observe signed videos of stories and answer questions about them. You will learn to identify main and supporting ideas from what you hear/observe. Thereafter, you will practise summarising the key points. Finally, you will retell/sign stories in your own words. The competences developed will help you to communicate better in English.
