Writing practice

Write a self-introduction using the following hints.

  • 1. Say/sign your name.
  • 2. Say/sign when you were born.
  • 3. Say/sign where you come from or where you live.
  • 4. Mention the name of your school
  • 5. Mention your class level.
  • 6. Say/sign ‘Thank you’ at the end.
