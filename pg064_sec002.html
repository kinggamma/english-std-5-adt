English Pupil’s Book Standard Five

(b)

Join the following sentences using ‘either…or’ or ‘neither… nor’.

Examples:

You can stay. You can also leave.

You can either stay or leave.

Asha does not praise them. She does not criticise them.

Asha neither praises nor criticises them.

1. She is not talkative. She is not reserved.

2. She does not like ugali. She does not like rice.

3. Juliet cannot ride a bicycle. She cannot ride a motorcycle.

4. Standard Five pupils will sit for weekly tests. Otherwise, they will sit for monthly tests.

5. You can wash utensils. You can clean the table.

6. You can speak English. You can speak Chinese.

7. We can eat now. We can eat later.
