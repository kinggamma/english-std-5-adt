Writing practice

(a) Imagine you accidentally broke your friend’s watch. Write an apology note to him or her.

1.Start your note with a greeting, for example, Dear [friend’s name].
2.Say sorry for what you did.
3.Explain what happened and take responsibility.
4.Express how you feel about it.
5.Offer to make it right, for example, by replacing the watch.
6.End your note politely, for example, Sincerely, [Your Name].

Example

Dear Amina,

I am very sorry for breaking your watch today. I should have handled it carefully. I feel bad because I know it was your favourite watch. I will bring you a new one tomorrow to make up for it. Please forgive me.

Sincerely,

Abdalah
