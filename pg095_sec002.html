Speaking/signing practice

(a) Practise saying tag questions.

  • 1. Jackline is from Shinyanga, isn’t she?
  • 2. Mbuke can speak Chinese and English, can’t she?
  • 3. Mr Mdolwa is not a doctor, is he?
  • 4. He shouldn’t say things like that, should he?
  • 5. We don’t like cheese and bread, do we?
  • 6. She can’t sing our school song, can she?
