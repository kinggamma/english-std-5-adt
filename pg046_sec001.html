Unit Five

Expressing completed and unfinished actions

Introduction

Using the correct verb form helps both the speaker and the listener to communicate clearly. In this unit, you will learn to express completed and unfinished actions using correct verb forms. You will practise in speech and writing to express completed and unfinished actions at present and in the past in real-life communication. The competences developed will enable you to describe actions and express your thoughts clearly, and understand others when they speak to you.

Think about how to express completed and unfinished actions.
