Write a thank-you note to someone who has helped or given you a gift. Follow the format below to organise your message.

Example format

  • 1. Greetings (Dear ,)
  • 2. Express gratitude (Say “Thank you” and mention what you are thankful for.)
  • 3. Explain why you appreciate it (How did it help you or make you feel?)
  • 4. Closing statement (End with a kind note or future hope.)
  • 5. Sign your name
