Unit Four

Expressing possession

Introduction

In daily communication, we use certain words to show our responsibilities and connections in our family, community, or nation. In this unit, you will learn to use possessive adjectives such as ‘my’, ‘you’, ‘his’, ‘her’, and ‘their’ to express possession. You will also learn to express possession using possessive pronouns such as ‘mine’, ‘yours’, ‘his’, ‘hers’ and ‘theirs’. The competences developed will enable you to express ownership and belonging in English.

Think about the words used to say something belongs to somebody or something.
