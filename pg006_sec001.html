Introduction

The English Pupil’s Book Standard Five has been prepared following the 2023 English Language Syllabus for Primary Education, Standards III–VI, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. This textbook is the second edition of the 2021 English Pupil’s Book Standard Five, published by TIE as per the 2018 Basic Education Syllabus for Standard III-VII.

This textbook consists of twelve units: Developing vocabulary, Comparing things, Describing locations and giving directions, Expressing possession, Expressing completed and unfinished actions, Using conjunctions, Using active and passive voices, Listening/observing for comprehension, Speaking English clearly and naturally/Signing words clearly, Expressing simple messages, Writing clear and concise messages, and Writing creatively. The book contains learning activities and real-life examples that will enable you to express yourself appropriately and fluently in both oral and written English. In addition, the book will help you expand your vocabulary for everyday communication.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz
