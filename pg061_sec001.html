Activity 6.1

Follow the instructions to play the ‘Either Or Game’.

Either jump or crouch down.

Either turn to your friend and say 'hi' or turn to your friend and say 'bye'.

Either put your hands on your head or put your head on the table.

Either stick out your tongue or touch your nose.

Either sit under the table or sit next to the door.

Either hop around the table or tip toe around the table.
