Unit Twelve

Writing creatively

Introduction

Writing creatively involves using imagination and creativity to write stories, poems, and other fun texts. It improves your creativity and writing skills. In this unit, you will learn how to create a journal to record things that inspire you at home, school, or on TV and radio. Then, you will pick one idea from your journal and write a short poem. You will also learn to use basic figures of speech like similes, metaphors, and personification in your poem. The competences developed will enable you to write creative texts, like journals about your daily life.

Think about improving your basic creative writing skills.
