Thank-you speech

Speaking/signing practice

(a) Read the following thank-you speech, memorise it, and present it to the class.

Good afternoon, friends!

I'm glad to see you. Thank you for coming.

I’m standing here for two reasons. The first is to thank you for accepting my invitation.

The second is to thank you for your active participation. I hope you enjoyed this gathering and had fun. Next time, things will be more exciting, God willing!

Thank you very much.
