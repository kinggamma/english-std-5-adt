(iii) Apology notes

Read the following dialogue and answer the questions that follow.

Teacher: Good morning, class! Today, we’re going to learn how to write an apology note. Can anyone tell me what an apology note is?

Pupil 1: It’s a note we write when we say sorry to someone.

Teacher: That’s right! An apology note is a short message we write when we want to say sorry for a mistake we’ve made. Why do you think writing an apology note is important?

Pupil 2: It helps to show that we regret what we did.

Pupil 3: It also helps to fix broken friendship or relationship.

Teacher: Exactly! An apology note helps us take responsibility for our actions and show that we’re truly sorry. Now, let’s talk about the form of an apology note. Do you remember the parts of a good note?

Pupil 4: It should have a greeting, a body, and a closing.

Teacher: Very good! The greeting is where we address the person we are apologising to, like Dear John or Dear Teacher. The body of the note is where we say what we are sorry for and why. We should also promise to do better next time. Finally, the closing is where we end politely with Sincerely or Yours truly, followed by name.

Pupil 5: So, in the body, we must explain why we’re sorry.

Teacher: Yes! And we should be honest and polite. Now, let’s talk about the stages of writing an apology note. The first stage is planning. What should we think about before writing?

Pupil 6: We should think about what we’ve done wrong.

Pupil 7: We should also decide how to express our feelings.

Teacher: Good answers! The second stage is drafting. This is when we write our first version of the note, following the correct form. The third stage is editing. What do we check during this stage?

Pupil 8: We check for spelling and grammatical errors.

Pupil 9: We also check if our message is clear and polite.

Teacher: Excellent! The last stage involves writing the final, neat copy. Now, let’s write an apology note together. Imagine you accidentally broke a friend’s pencil. How’d you get started?

Pupil 10: I’d write, “Dear [Friend’s Name], I am very sorry for breaking your pencil.”

Teacher: Great! Now, continue by explaining why it happened and what you’ll do to make it right. Let’s write our notes and then share them!

Questions

1. What is an apology note?

2. When do you need to write an apology note?

3. What are the three main parts you will include in the apology note?

4. What will you include in the greeting of the apology note?

5. What will you write in the body of the apology note?

6. How will you end the apology note?
