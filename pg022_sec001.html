Reading practice

(a) Read the following story and answer the questions that follow.

Comparisons on a walk

One day, Anna and her friends went for a walk in the forest. They saw three rocks in the forest. The first rock was big, the second was bigger, but the third was the biggest. It was so large that they could all sit on it like a bench.

Nearby, there were three trees: one tree was small, another was smaller, and the last was the smallest. It was so tiny that it looked more like a garden plant.

Anna smiled and said, “We saw big, bigger, and the biggest rocks as well as small, smaller, and the smallest trees.”

Questions

1. Which rock was the biggest?

2. What is the comparative form of the word ‘big’?

3. Which tree was the smallest?

4. What is the superlative form of the word ‘small’?

5. Write three forms of the word ‘big’ as used in the story.

6. Write three forms of the word ‘small’ as used in the story.

7. Why did the children say the last rock was the biggest?
