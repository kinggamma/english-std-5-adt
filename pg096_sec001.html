Dialogue 2: At Home

Mother: You haven’t finished your homework, have you?

Zubeda: Yes, I have, mama. It wasn’t too difficult.

Mother: It wasn’t! But you haven’t checked your answers, have you?

Zubeda: I checked them twice! You trust me, don’t you?

Mother: Of course, I do. But you don’t always take your time with your work.

Zubeda: Not this time, mama. I took my time. You’re not disappointed in me, are you?

Mother: Certainly not! You don’t have a test tomorrow, do you?

Zubeda: Yes, I do and I am prepared for it.

Mother: You won’t sleep late tonight, will you?

Zubeda: No, I won’t. I don’t want to be tired in the morning.
