Unit Ten

Expressing simple messages

Introduction

In our daily lives, we often talk about things around us, share ideas, and give information to others. In this unit, you will learn to speak/sign clearly and correctly by making simple statements about objects and ideas that can be judged as true or false. You will also practise giving short speeches about yourself, your family, and familiar topics like your pet, a birthday party, or a wedding. The competences developed will enable you to communicate clearly and effectively.
