Comparing the quantity of things or living organisms

(a) Study the pictures. Read the dialogue below the pictures. The dialogue is about Saumu and James exploring things and pictures in their classroom.

books
pens
animals

Saumu: (Looking at the table) Wow, James, look at this! There are two books on the table.

James: Yes, I can see them! But take a look at the shirt pocket. There are more pens in it than there are books on the table.

Saumu: You’re right! There are three pens in the pocket and only two books on the table. So, the pocket has more pens than there are books on the table.

James: And look at the animals. There are four animals!

Saumu: Yeah! There are more animals in the picture than books on the table or pens in the shirt pocket!

James: Right! Four animals, three pens, and two books. So, the pictures show more animals than pens and books.

Saumu: Exactly! There are more animals than pens or books.

James: I agree. And there are also more pens and animals than books.

Saumu: Learning with pictures is fun!

James: It sure is! Now I understand how to use “morethan” when we compare things.
